# Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to maintain a professional standard of conduct. Treat others respectfully; do not insult, harass, demean, or intentionally provoke contributors or users.

Technical disagreement is normal and expected. Critique ideas, designs, and code—not people.

Project spaces are for work on Journeyman. Political or religious advocacy, campaigning, or debate unrelated to the project is out of scope.

Assume good faith where reasonable. Keep discussions focused on the technical problem, the design, or the implementation at hand.

Repeated behaviour that materially disrupts constructive collaboration may result in maintainers moderating discussions or restricting participation.
